Educare Skill Academy — Full CRM Development Prompt

1. Project Overview

Build a production-ready, multi-office CRM and business management platform for Educare Skill Academy.

Educare Skill Academy provides:

1. Professional course coaching/training
2. Diploma/B.Tech/ITI-related coaching
3. Internship services
4. College/university admission assistance
5. Career counselling
6. Job placement assistance
7. Company/job matching
8. Student payment and EMI management

The CRM must manage the complete customer lifecycle:

LEAD
→ CALLING
→ COUNSELLING
→ APPOINTMENT
→ ENROLLMENT
→ PAYMENT / EMI
→ COURSE
→ BATCH
→ TRAINING / COACHING
→ JOB ELIGIBILITY
→ JOB MATCHING
→ INTERVIEW
→ SELECTION
→ OFFER
→ JOINING
→ PLACEMENT COMPLETED
→ FOLLOW-UP
→ ACCOUNTS / FINANCE

College admission is a parallel business workflow:

LEAD
→ COUNSELLING
→ COLLEGE / COURSE SELECTION
→ APPLICATION
→ ADMISSION
→ PAYMENT
→ SEMESTER PAYMENT
→ ADMISSION COMPLETED

The system must be designed as a modular, scalable CRM, not as a simple CRUD application.

46. Important Business Rule

Never use manually entered totals as the primary source of financial truth.

For example:

Enrollment Fee = ₹50,000
Discount = ₹5,000

Final Fee = ₹45,000

Payments:
₹10,000
₹15,000

Paid = ₹25,000
Due = ₹20,000

The backend should calculate:

Paid = SUM(valid payments)

Due = Final Fee - Paid

Do not allow users to directly edit "Paid" or "Due" without an authorized adjustment workflow.

A student can have multiple interviews.

Financial transactions must be auditable.

Office restrictions must be enforced on backend APIs.

Critical records should not be hard deleted.

Do not hardcode course names into application logic.

Do not hardcode GST rates.

Do not trust frontend permissions.

Do not calculate financial balances only in React.

Build reusable components instead of duplicating code.
