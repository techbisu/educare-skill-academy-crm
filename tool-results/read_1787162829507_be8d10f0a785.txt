     1→Educare Skill Academy — Full CRM Development Prompt
     2→
     3→1. Project Overview
     4→
     5→Build a production-ready, multi-office CRM and business management platform for Educare Skill Academy.
     6→
     7→Educare Skill Academy provides:
     8→
     9→1. Professional course coaching/training
    10→2. Diploma/B.Tech/ITI-related coaching
    11→3. Internship services
    12→4. College/university admission assistance
    13→5. Career counselling
    14→6. Job placement assistance
    15→7. Company/job matching
    16→8. Student payment and EMI management
    17→
    18→The CRM must manage the complete customer lifecycle:
    19→
    20→LEAD
    21→→ CALLING
    22→→ COUNSELLING
    23→→ APPOINTMENT
    24→→ ENROLLMENT
    25→→ PAYMENT / EMI
    26→→ COURSE
    27→→ BATCH
    28→→ TRAINING / COACHING
    29→→ JOB ELIGIBILITY
    30→→ JOB MATCHING
    31→→ INTERVIEW
    32→→ SELECTION
    33→→ OFFER
    34→→ JOINING
    35→→ PLACEMENT COMPLETED
    36→→ FOLLOW-UP
    37→→ ACCOUNTS / FINANCE
    38→
    39→College admission is a parallel business workflow:
    40→
    41→LEAD
    42→→ COUNSELLING
    43→→ COLLEGE / COURSE SELECTION
    44→→ APPLICATION
    45→→ ADMISSION
    46→→ PAYMENT
    47→→ SEMESTER PAYMENT
    48→→ ADMISSION COMPLETED
    49→
    50→The system must be designed as a modular, scalable CRM, not as a simple CRUD application.

  1616→46. Important Business Rule
  1617→
  1618→Never use manually entered totals as the primary source of financial truth.
  1619→
  1620→For example:
  1621→
  1622→Enrollment Fee = ₹50,000
  1623→Discount = ₹5,000
  1624→
  1625→Final Fee = ₹45,000
  1626→
  1627→Payments:
  1628→₹10,000
  1629→₹15,000
  1630→
  1631→Paid = ₹25,000
  1632→Due = ₹20,000
  1633→
  1634→The backend should calculate:
  1635→
  1636→Paid = SUM(valid payments)
  1637→
  1638→Due = Final Fee - Paid
  1639→
  1640→Do not allow users to directly edit "Paid" or "Due" without an authorized adjustment workflow.
